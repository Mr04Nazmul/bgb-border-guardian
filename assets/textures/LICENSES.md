# Landscape materials

The files listed in SOURCES.json are downloaded from the original Poly Haven API responses, verified against the publisher's MD5, and recorded with SHA-256. All are CC0-1.0: https://polyhaven.com/license

- forest_ground_04: diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness
- leafy_grass: diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness
- corrugated_iron_02: diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness
- red_brick_03: diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness

foliage.png is original procedurally drawn leaf/branch artwork, MIT licensed under the project LICENSE. Terrain, tea bushes, grass, trees, architecture and horizon meshes are original procedural geometry under that license.

These are generic surface textures, not photographs of Bangladesh or evidence of an exact real location. The setting is fictional and inspired by Sylhet's broad landscape characteristics. No actual military facility or security layout is reconstructed.
